    Weights are visible and editable so the tool imposes no hidden value judgment. Defaults reflect a regulated-industry posture: risk and responsible-design carry more weight.
    Weights are normalised to 100%.
    How to read this. This is a structured aid to human judgment, not a decision-maker. It does not use an AI model to score your use case; it applies a fixed, visible rule set, so the same inputs always give the same result and every point can be traced back to an answer you selected. The verdict reflects what you entered, which is an estimate, and it is a self-assessment, so a second independent reviewer reduces individual bias. It does not replace a Data Protection Impact Assessment, GxP validation, security review, fairness testing, or sign-off from accountable owners. Guardrails can only lower a verdict, never raise it, and the guardrails depend only on the high-signal inputs shown, so a rapid triage cannot hide a red line.
    Methodology v1.0 · informed by the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, the OECD AI Principles, the EU AI Act risk tiers, Good Machine Learning Practice, and WHO guidance on AI for health · calibrated for regulated clinical and digital-health settings.
